Practical 1: Employee Sales Analysis in Excel

Use Microsoft Excel to analyze employee sales, targets, attendance and customer ratings. Complete the practical using Excel formulas and logical functions.

Practical Questions

1. Total Sales
Calculate the total monthly sales of all employees using SUM.
2. Average Sales
Calculate the average monthly sales using AVERAGE.
3. Number of Employees
Find the total number of employees using COUNT.
4. Minimum Sales
Find the lowest monthly sales using MIN.
5. Maximum Sales
Find the highest monthly sales using MAX.
6. Target Achievement – IF
Create a Target Status column. Display "Achieved" if sales are equal to or greater than target; otherwise display "Not Achieved".
7. Bonus Eligibility – IF + AND
Give "Bonus" only when sales are equal to or greater than target AND attendance is at least 80%.
8. Special Recognition – IF + OR
Display "Yes" if sales are at least ₹1,50,000 OR customer rating is at least 4.8.
